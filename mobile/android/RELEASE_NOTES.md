**OksaSDR for Android — beta 65.** A FlexRadio client for Android phones and tablets, built on the [AetherSDR](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR) engine: the same app as on the iPhone and iPad (TestFlight), from the same code.

### New in beta 65
- **4O3A Power Genius XL and Tuner Genius XL** (the user: both working, not only their meters): a PGXL or TGXL connected to the radio shows on the Amp tab by itself. The radio reports it and relays its state, power, SWR and Operate / Standby (over SmartLink too); the phone also reads it directly at its address (ports 9008 / 9010, same network or VPN). The amplifier: its state, Forward (0-2000 W, red from 1500), SWR, Drive, current, supply, PA and harmonic-load temperatures, its ports, an alert; **Standby / Operate** and the **fan**. The tuner: its state, forward power and SWR, **Standby / Bypass / Operate**, **Tune** (the tuner keys the radio itself and keys or holds its amplifier as its utility says; Stop tuning ends it), the relays **C1 / L / C2** a step at a time and the **antenna** switch of a 1x3 model. Radio Setup › Amp: **Control** for each (off until turned on), an address of your own, and the device's **remote code** when it asks (kept in the Android Keystore once accepted). Every change asks first; the devices guard their own SWR, power and temperature, and the tuner's relays and antenna wait while the radio transmits. The meters' Amp readings come from a PGXL when no RF2K-S is set up, and with a PGXL in Operate the Power meter shows its output on 2 kW. Tested against a stand-in that replays real devices' frames (the remote code, Standby / Operate, the fan, relays, antenna, a tune, keyed readings); real devices still to verify.
- **APD on a FLEX-8000 or Aurora** (shown only when the radio offers it): the TX tab's **APD Off / On** and whether it is correcting, and in Radio Setup › Transmit the **sampler port** per TX antenna (INTERNAL, or RX_A / RX_B / XVTA / XVTB for an external amplifier's sample) and the **equalizer reset**, as the desktop's.
- **The Power meter follows the radio** (thanks Sloan Davis, whose Aurora's meter turned red past 100 W): 0-120 W as before, 0-600 W red from 500 W on an Aurora or a radio over 100 W, a QRP radio's own scale.
- **The radio list shows each address on a line of its own** under the radio's name (thanks Mike VA3MW).

The same firmware refuses the commands for multiFLEX, binaural receive and the low-latency digital filters ("could not parse"), so these show as the radio reports them.
- **The transmit lock counts down** (thanks Larry Averbeck, Galaxy S26 Ultra): armed, OA 5:00 on PTT now counts down from the arming or the last transmission and goes back to SB after five minutes without one (the timer never ran). And **TX › At start: Hold to arm**: with the lock off, the app starts in SB until PTT, KEY, TUNE or CQ is held a second once.
- **View › Zoom buttons: Left / Right** (thanks Larry): Right mirrors the display's corner buttons for a right hand: − + at the right edge, Band and B/S toward the middle, + Pan and + Slice at the left.
- **Scroll bars wide enough to take, and lists inside the window** (thanks Larry): the bars are 8 px, always draggable and shown while the content overflows; the profile and CTCSS lists keep clear of the phone's status and navigation bars and scroll when longer than the room.

### Still new from beta 62
- **Control of the RF-KIT RF2K-S** (step 2; thanks Sebastian DG5CST, whose amplifier showed beta 61's readings right): turn on Radio Setup › Amp › **Control the amplifier** (off until you do; Forget turns it off again), and the Amp tab gets **Mode** (Standby / Operate, the present one dark), **Antenna** (the internal antennas 1-4 the amplifier has set up; EXT lights when its external antenna is in use, shown, not switched) and **Reset error**. Each asks first, naming the change, and goes only with the button that names it; nothing goes while the radio transmits (an open question closes by itself when the radio keys, the link drops or Control goes off); one change at a time; the tab changes only when the amplifier reads the new state back, a refusal shows the amplifier's reason, and a lost answer says the state is unknown until it is read again. Tested against a stand-in of the API by the real buttons, and keyed on a FLEX for the transmit lockout.
- **A peak marker on the amplifier's Forward bar**: the highest reading of the last 3 seconds, as the desktop's amplifier panel, and the amplifier is read four times a second while the radio transmits (twice otherwise), so the power follows the keying (Sebastian's idea).
- **Keying an amplifier from Radio Setup › Transmit**, as SmartSDR's Radio Setup has it, so no other program is needed: **Keying outputs: delay after PTT** (RCA TX1, TX2, TX3 and ACC TX, in ms; the radio's signal waits for TX delay, e.g. TX1 20 ms, TX delay 30 ms), the **TX REQ inputs** (RCA and ACC, active low or active high; active high asks first, as an open input then keys the radio), and the **TX band settings**: pick a band, then its RF and tune power, which outputs close there (the amplifier's PTT only on the bands it covers), which TX REQ inputs work there, PTT inhibit and hardware ALC. Kept in the radio's TX profile. Every setting was written from the page and read back on a FLEX-6600M. With them **Inhibit during TUNE** (this device's setting, as the desktop's): the outputs held open while TUNE or the ATU transmits, so tuning does not key the amplifier; and **PTT mic override** on radios with SmartSDR 4.3.
- **USB cables in Radio Setup › USB**, as SmartSDR's USB Cables: the USB-serial (FTDI) cables in the radio's USB ports that give an amplifier, a tuner or an antenna switch the frequency or the band. Each cable's name, on / off and type: **CAT** (the frequency as CAT text, from the TX slice, the active slice, a pan, an antenna or a slice by letter, auto report, and the port's speed, data bits, parity, stop bits and flow control), **BCD** (the band as BCD lines, HF / VHF / both, active high or low), **Bit** (eight lines, each for a band or a frequency range, active high or low, optionally only while transmitting with PTT and TX delays), **LDPA** (FlexRadio's 2 m / 4 m amplifier) and **passthrough**. A cable the radio remembers but is unplugged can be forgotten. The radio answers a cable setting with an OK, not a status, so the page shows it once taken.
- **The amplifier's readings on every meter**: tap a meter, and under **Amplifier** pick Power, Refl, SWR, Temp, Volts or Current, for receive and for transmit (Sebastian asked for the output on the small right meter). The temperature turns red from 70 °C (the RF2K-S goes to standby at 72 °C).

### Still new from beta 61
- **Amplifier, read only (first step): an RF-KIT RF2K-S** over its own network port (its REST API; thanks Sebastian DG5CST). Enter its address in Radio Setup › **Amp** (the IP address or host name, `:port` after it when not 8080), and Controls gets an **Amp** tab with what the amplifier reports, twice a second: **OPERATE** / **STANDBY**, forward and reflected power and SWR on the amplifier's own scales, the PA's temperature, voltage and current, band and frequency, tuner, antenna, interface, status and an error if it reports one. The phone reaches the amplifier directly, on the same network or over your VPN (not through the radio, not over SmartLink), only while the app is on screen and connected to a radio. Beta 61 sent nothing that changes the amplifier (the control is beta 62's, above). Tested against a stand-in of the API: readings, a lost link, a wrong device at the address, a slow answer, the switch off, the app in the background; not one command sent.

### Still new from beta 60
- **PSK31 and PSK63 on the Digi tab** (the Mode row: RTTY, PSK31, PSK63), in DIGU as the standard has them: tune the slice about a kilohertz below the signals (14.0695 for 14.070-14.073), tap a signal on the display and the decoder goes to it (a green band marks where it listens, with its audio frequency), **AFC** follows it within 25 Hz, −10 / +10 move it by hand. The same calls heard, macros (F1-F8), **Text to send**, **Stop** and **Log QSO** as for RTTY (ADIF PSK with the submode PSK31 / PSK63, on the signal's frequency); text goes out on the frequency received, as fldigi sends, and PSK keeps lower case. A PSK spot puts the tab in PSK and tunes to the signal. The decoder copies PSK31 down to about -12 dB SNR and leaves a station 100 Hz away alone. Tested on the air: a PSK31 QSO at 25 W with F6FPU near Lyon.
- **Macro keys on a keyboard without F-keys**: Ctrl with 1-9 and 0 works as F1-F10 (⌘ on an iPad's Magic Keyboard) for the Digi macros, the CW memories and the voice keyer.
- **RTTY reads weak signals better**: the decoder's bit clock samples mid-bit (it drifted toward the bit edges); in tests at -3 dB SNR the errors fell from 1-4 % to under 1 %.
- A macro's **Default** puts the original in the editor to see; **Save** keeps it. The Digi tab's **Log QSO** logs the QSO's start (when its call was picked), not the moment Log is pressed.

### Still new from beta 58
- **RTTY on either sideband, logged and spotted right:** received in DIGL or DIGU, a QSO you log goes in as RTTY on the signal's mark frequency, as in the radio's RTTY mode (it went in as DATA on the carrier, 2 kHz off), and a tap on an RTTY spot keeps DIGL / DIGU, tuning so the spot's mark lands on the mark tone. ### Install or update
1. Download `OksaSDR-android-beta60.apk` below on the phone (about 150 MB).
2. Open it; allow installing from your browser or file manager when Android asks. If Chrome keeps the download waiting, open ⋮ → *Downloads* → *Keep*.
3. Beta 60 installs over earlier betas and keeps your settings. If you have one of the earlier hand-delivered test APKs, uninstall it first (different signing key).

Android 9 or newer, 64-bit ARM. On the first connect, allow notifications, the microphone and "no battery optimization" so receive keeps going in the background and PTT can transmit.
