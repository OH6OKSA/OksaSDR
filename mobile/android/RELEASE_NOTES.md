**OksaSDR for Android — beta 75.** A FlexRadio client for Android phones and tablets, built on the [AetherSDR](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR) engine: the same app as on the iPhone and iPad (TestFlight), from the same code.

### New in beta 75
- **Auto AGC-T** (the developer's wish, as Thetis has it): **Auto** under the AGC-T slider (tap the AGC in the info row). On a radio with SmartSDR 4.3.16 and FlexRadio's paid Early Access licence it turns the radio's own Auto AGC-T on and off. On every other radio the app measures it: on a clear spot (Auto says if a signal is in the passband) it steps AGC-T from 100 down for about 13 s, listens to the receive audio and sets AGC-T at the knee, where the noise starts to drop (FlexRadio's own advice, by ear). The value is kept for the band and the mode group (voice, CW, data) and set again whenever you come back to them; moving the slider by hand lets it go; **Stop** puts AGC-T back. Measured on the FLEX: 52-60 on quiet spots on 20 m and 15 m at night, the same within one step between two sweeps; a broadcast station and a weak carrier in the passband refused.
- **The TX tag's power on narrow chips** (the developer's OnePlus outer screen, two bars at both meters and two slices: the tag showed only TX): "TX" over the power in small type now comes before that.
- **The Macros button's repeat mark** was an empty box on Android (⟳ is in none of its fonts): now ↻.

### Still new from beta 73
- **App setups** (thanks Larry Averbeck W9FLA: a setup for each way of operating, shared between devices): **Controls › Prof › App setups** saves the app's own settings under a name (**Save as…**, **Save**); picking a setup in the list switches to it at once. What reads its setting only at start (the amplifier, the 4O3A devices, the DX cluster) follows at the next start, and the list says so. The radio's own settings stay in its profiles above.
- **Macros › Repeat** (thanks Robin Bishop: for contests and POTA): with **Repeat** on, the voice keyer slot or CW memory you tap is sent again after a gap you set with − / + (2-60 s, 5 s at first), until PTT, KEY, the paddles, **Stop**, any key or another slot. The switch starts off at each launch and goes off when the radio disconnects. Tested on the FLEX at 10 W with a CW memory.
- **SNR on the meters** (thanks Scott L Stanford): while RADE runs, each meter's receive choices offer **SNR**, the RADE decoder's own, on 0-20 dB (*–* until it is synced); the choice comes back the next time RADE is on.
- **SmartSDR 4.3's PTT source list**: the radio reports every latched PTT source, e.g. *MIC,VOX*; the app took that as no hardware PTT, so a press of the microphone's PTT or a footswitch with VOX off could turn the TX meters and the transmit audio gate off during the transmission. ### Still new from beta 69

- **A peak marker on the amplifier's Forward bar**: the highest reading of the last 3 seconds, as the desktop's amplifier panel, and the amplifier is read four times a second while the radio transmits (twice otherwise), so the power follows the keying (Sebastian's idea).
- **Keying an amplifier from Radio Setup › Transmit**, as SmartSDR's Radio Setup has it, so no other program is needed: **Keying outputs: delay after PTT** (RCA TX1, TX2, TX3 and ACC TX, in ms; the radio's signal waits for TX delay, e.g. TX1 20 ms, TX delay 30 ms), the **TX REQ inputs** (RCA and ACC, active low or active high; active high asks first, as an open input then keys the radio), and the **TX band settings**: pick a band, then its RF and tune power, which outputs close there (the amplifier's PTT only on the bands it covers), which TX REQ inputs work there, PTT inhibit and hardware ALC. Kept in the radio's TX profile. Every setting was written from the page and read back on a FLEX-6600M. With them **Inhibit during TUNE** (this device's setting, as the desktop's): the outputs held open while TUNE or the ATU transmits, so tuning does not key the amplifier; and **PTT mic override** on radios with SmartSDR 4.3.
- **USB cables in Radio Setup › USB**, as SmartSDR's USB Cables: the USB-serial (FTDI) cables in the radio's USB ports that give an amplifier, a tuner or an antenna switch the frequency or the band. Each cable's name, on / off and type: **CAT** (the frequency as CAT text, from the TX slice, the active slice, a pan, an antenna or a slice by letter, auto report, and the port's speed, data bits, parity, stop bits and flow control), **BCD** (the band as BCD lines, HF / VHF / both, active high or low), **Bit** (eight lines, each for a band or a frequency range, active high or low, optionally only while transmitting with PTT and TX delays), **LDPA** (FlexRadio's 2 m / 4 m amplifier) and **passthrough**. A cable the radio remembers but is unplugged can be forgotten. The radio answers a cable setting with an OK, not a status, so the page shows it once taken.
- **The amplifier's readings on every meter**: tap a meter, and under **Amplifier** pick Power, Refl, SWR, Temp, Volts or Current, for receive and for transmit (Sebastian asked for the output on the small right meter). The temperature turns red from 70 °C (the RF2K-S goes to standby at 72 °C).

### Install or update
1. Download `OksaSDR-android-beta60.apk` below on the phone (about 150 MB).
2. Open it; allow installing from your browser or file manager when Android asks. If Chrome keeps the download waiting, open ⋮ → *Downloads* → *Keep*.
3. Beta 60 installs over earlier betas and keeps your settings. If you have one of the earlier hand-delivered test APKs, uninstall it first (different signing key).

Android 9 or newer, 64-bit ARM. On the first connect, allow notifications, the microphone and "no battery optimization" so receive keeps going in the background and PTT can transmit.
