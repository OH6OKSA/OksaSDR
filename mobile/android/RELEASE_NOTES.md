**OksaSDR for Android — beta 85.** A FlexRadio client for Android phones and tablets, built on the [AetherSDR](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR) engine: the same app as on the iPhone and iPad (TestFlight), from the same code.

### New in beta 85
- **Spelling the call by voice works on more phones**: beta 84's voice button asked the phone's own on-device recogniser, and on a phone without its English model (one set to another language) it only said "English speech recognition is not installed". Now the recording goes to the phone's default recogniser (Google's, over the network) instead, and the English model is asked for, so later the phone can do it on its own. While listening only this device's audio goes quiet (beta 84 muted the slice on the radio, for every station on it), and a press the system cancels ends the listening as letting go does.
- **Type the RIT or XIT offset** (thanks Sebastian Schumann DG5CST): a tap on the value in the DSP tab's RIT or XIT row opens a field: *1.34* (kHz) or *-0.5*, a whole number of 10 or more as Hz (*1340*), or −1 / +1 / +2 / +3 / +5 kHz; *Reset* sets 0, and a value set turns it on. **Hold SPLIT › Other…** splits by any distance, "up 1.34" with a second slice or by XIT.
- **Big buttons: the mode** at the top right opens the mode chooser, as on the main screen.

### Still new from beta 84
- **Spell the call by voice** (idea: Michael Walker): **🎤** beside the call field on the Log tab and in Big buttons' log. Hold it, spell the call with the international alphabet (*Victor Alpha Three Mike Whiskey*, *niner*, *stroke portable*) and let go; a quick tap listens until a second tap. Android's own speech recognition (on the device where it offers that) turns it into text, the call goes into the field and a report said after it (*five nine*) into Sent; nothing is logged until you tap Log. It mutes the radio's audio here while listening and does not work while transmitting. Needs Android 13 or later; the first time Android may download its English speech model. National alphabets may follow.
- **Edit or delete a logged contact** (thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF): a tap on one in the Log tab's list opens it: the call, date and time, frequency, mode, reports, name, comment and the parks, then *Save*, or *Delete* and *Delete it*. It changes this device's log; a copy that already went to Wavelog / Cloudlog, the QRZ.com logbook, the shared ADIF file or Logger32 is not changed there (the dialog names them).
- **× in the call field** clears it, and a **Name** field beside Comment takes the looked-up first name until you type one; it is logged as ADIF NAME (thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF).

### Still new from beta 83
- **POTA self-spot from the Log tab** (thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF): with **My park** set to POTA and your park's reference typed, **Self-spot** beside it shows what goes out (*OH6OKSA at US-1234, 14285.0 kHz SSB*, the transmit slice's frequency and mode, FT8 or FT4 while FTx runs), takes a comment if you like, and on **Spot** posts it to pota.app as a public spot under your call; POTA's answer shows under the row. With two parks (*US-1234, US-5678*) it asks which. At most one spot in 30 s, and nothing is sent by itself. (pota.app's spot interface is unofficial: if POTA changes it, the answer says so.)
- **Big buttons › Log** (thanks Michael Walker): **Log** beside the mode opens the call in big type and the two reports with big **−** / **+** (59, 599 or the FTx report), then **Log** (with My park, as on the Log tab; the call is the Log tab's too). **Cancel** goes back, PTT stays at the bottom, and with a keyboard Return logs and Esc cancels.
- **The KPA1500's Tune in STANDBY** (thanks Rich Yost N2RY): Rich's log showed the tune carrier going out at about 775 W for the last 0.7 s of each tune: once the ATU had tuned, the amplifier was back in OPERATE and amplified the carrier the app still held to read the SWR. Tune now puts the amplifier in STANDBY first, as the RF2K-S's Tune does, stops the carrier if it ever reads OPERATE, and after the tune says *Put it in OPERATE when you are ready*. The other amplifiers were checked for the same: the RF2K-S already tunes in STANDBY, the Tuner Genius XL keys the radio itself, and the radio's own TUNE keeps the outputs chosen under Radio Setup › Transmit › *Inhibit during TUNE* open.
- **Receive audio in order** (upstream AetherSDR's fix for #6051): audio packets that arrive out of order, as they often do over a VPN, LTE or Wi-Fi, are put back in order before they are played (a gap is waited for up to 30 ms); before, a packet that came late sounded like a lost one. Also from upstream: a late spectrum or waterfall packet no longer drops the frame being drawn, and a small packet to the radio every 5 s keeps a firewall's return path for the spectrum and audio open.
- **RADE and the voice keyer** (thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF): F1–F12 played the radio's own voice keyer (SmartSDR+'s DVK) while RADE was on, and the radio sends that as analog audio, so it went out as plain SSB on a RADE frequency. They now say why and do not play it there; the app's own recordings (on a radio without SmartSDR+) go out as RADE like your voice.
- (iPhone, iPad and Mac only: **MIDI Learn names a control another function has** (thanks Rich Yost N2RY): Radio Setup › MIDI asks *Replace* or *Learn again* instead of silently taking it over. MIDI controllers come to Android later.)

### Still new from beta 82
- **Spot from the Log tab** (thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF): **Spot** beside Log sends the call to the DX cluster the app is logged in to (Controls › View › DX cluster), at the slice frequency. It asks first and shows the exact line that goes out (*DX 14154.0 K1ABC SSB*), as it is a public post under your call; the remark is the mode, with their park first when you have set one (*POTA US-1234 SSB*), and you can change it. Under the row: *Spotted on the cluster* when the spot comes back, the cluster's own answer if it refuses it, or *Not seen back* after 30 s. Without the cluster Spot is dimmed and a tap says where to turn it on.
- **Big buttons: Step in two halves and a larger Exit** (thanks Michael Walker): the left half of the Step bar takes a smaller step (▼), the right half a larger one (▲), the step shown across the middle; at the smallest or largest step its half is dimmed. **Exit** is as large as the slice buttons beside it, and leaves after half the hold it needed.
- **Noise line in the AGC panel**: the noise floor line's switch was only in Controls › View, easy to miss; now it is also **Noise line** under Auto and Follow in the AGC panel (tap the RF GAIN label or the AGC in the info row). The line is thicker in 2D, where it sank into the trace's own noise.
- **The Tuner Genius XL first on the Amp tab** (thanks Hanno Vogels DK3HV): above the Power Genius XL. A Tune the radio refuses while the app's own link to the tuner is down now says so (it went through the radio; with the link up Tune goes to the tuner directly) and what to check, not only the radio's code.
- **SPLIT 1 kHz up in DIGU, DIGL and RTTY**, as in CW (5 kHz put an FT8 transmit slice outside the FT8 segment); voice stays 5 kHz, and holding SPLIT still chooses.
- (iPhone, iPad and Mac only: **F1–F12 and Stop on a MIDI controller's buttons**, Radio Setup › MIDI › Macros, thanks Christoph Steinert DM5HF. MIDI controllers come to Android later.)

### Still new from beta 81
- **AGC-T follows the noise** (AGC panel › **Follow**, as Thetis's Auto AGC): from Auto's measurement of each band and mode, AGC-T moves as the noise floor rises and falls, at dusk on 80 m, at sunrise on 40 m or on another antenna. It waits, and says why, until Auto has measured the band and mode, with a filter much wider or narrower than measured with, in AGC off, while you transmit and in FM. FM has no AGC on the radio, so Auto is dimmed there.
- **Noise floor line** (Controls › View): the noise floor the app reads as a dashed yellow line across the spectrum with its dBm, in 2D and 3D. Off by default.
- The passband stays in view as the display follows your tuning (an LSB slice tuned down showed only part of its filter at a narrow span).
- The Elecraft KPA1500's Tune ends when the amplifier says its tune is over, and the log says each second what it showed (thanks Rich Yost N2RY).
- (iPhone, iPad and Mac only: pairing a Bluetooth MIDI controller no longer crashes on iOS 27.)
