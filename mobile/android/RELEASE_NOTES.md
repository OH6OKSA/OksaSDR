**OksaSDR for Android — beta 32.** A FlexRadio client for Android phones and tablets, built on the [AetherSDR](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR) engine: the same app as on the iPhone and iPad (TestFlight), from the same code.

### New in beta 32
- **CQ POTA filter on the FTx tab** (*Show › CQ POTA*): only park activators calling `CQ POTA` (thanks Ryland WW4GA). A contact with one goes into the log with SIG POTA.
- **Heard in CW on the Log tab:** with the CW decoder on, the calls it heard (after *DE*, or twice) are offered under the Call field; a tap fills it in.

### Still new from beta 31
- **RADE receive fixed** on DAX channels 2 and 3, and a quick PTT tap sends RADE's end of over with your callsign.
- **FreeDV station list by activity** (transmitting now first, then *TX 3 min ago*); **New call** colours and filter on the FTx tab; **big logbooks** in little memory; **a keyboard on the phone** tunes and keys as on a tablet; the slice line is left out in voice modes.

Earlier betas (14–30): big ADIF imports in seconds, two-tap SmartLink Sign out; sliders, keypads, ⋯ menu and text fields the same on every device (thanks Richard EI9JE); meters Analog / Bar / Two bars with scales and peak hold, the white dashed slice line, FT8 / FT4 transmit fixed and ending when the slice goes back to voice, RADE fixes; the FreeDV tab with FreeDV Reporter (asked by Scott W5SLS), FTx and one-size control buttons, the beta number in brackets, the DX cluster status line, the display going on while the app is on screen but not in focus; a swipe in the open controls drawer no longer tunes, PTT in DIGU / DIGL says why it does not transmit, three meters while transmitting (asked by Mike VA3MW); FT8 and FT4 in the app (Controls › FTx) on every DIGU slice with WSJT-X colours, Keep CQ, Last TX and Free, PSK Reporter; Wavelog / Cloudlog; transverter setup; RADE digital voice; the log in Files › Documents › OksaSDR.

### Install or update
1. Download `OksaSDR-android-beta32.apk` below on the phone (about 150 MB).
2. Open it; allow installing from your browser or file manager when Android asks. If Chrome keeps the download waiting, open ⋮ → *Downloads* → *Keep*.
3. Beta 32 installs over earlier betas and keeps your settings. If you have one of the earlier hand-delivered test APKs, uninstall it first (different signing key).

Android 9 or newer, 64-bit ARM. On the first connect, allow notifications, the microphone and "no battery optimization" so receive keeps going in the background and PTT can transmit.
