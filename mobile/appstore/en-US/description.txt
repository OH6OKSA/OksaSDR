OksaSDR turns an iPhone, iPad or Apple silicon Mac into a control head for FlexRadio FLEX-6000, FLEX-8000 and Aurora software-defined transceivers. Operate on your home network, from anywhere over SmartLink, or through your own VPN.

RECEIVE AND TUNE
• Panadapter and waterfall in 2D or 3D, tuned by touch, a keyboard or a MIDI controller
• Several slices and panadapters, each band on a panadapter of its own
• Modes, filters, AGC, noise reduction, notch filters, split, RIT and XIT
• Band stack, memories, DX cluster spots on the display
• Meters as analog dials, a bar or two bars

TRANSMIT
• Voice with the device's microphone or a Bluetooth headset, PTT on the screen or the Space bar
• CW with a keyer, memories and a decoder
• A voice keyer with your recorded messages
• TUNE, the radio's ATU and a transmit lock

DIGITAL MODES IN THE APP
• FT8 and FT4 decoding and QSOs, on several slices at once, with PSK Reporter
• RTTY and PSK
• RADE digital voice with FreeDV Reporter
No computer, DAX or WSJT-X needed.

LOGGING
• A log on the device with QRZ.com lookups, ADIF import and export, and POTA
• Each QSO also to Wavelog, Cloudlog, the QRZ.com Logbook, Logger32 or a logging program on your network

FOR THE STATION
• Radio Setup for the radio's own settings, TX band settings and accessories
• 4O3A Power Genius XL, Tuner Genius XL and Antenna Genius, Elecraft KPA1500 and RF-KIT RF2K-S amplifiers, MIDI controllers
• Big buttons: a screen of a few large controls for the car or for gloves

Requires a FlexRadio FLEX-6000, FLEX-8000 or Aurora series transceiver, and an amateur radio licence to transmit. A built-in receive-only demo radio lets you try the app without one.
