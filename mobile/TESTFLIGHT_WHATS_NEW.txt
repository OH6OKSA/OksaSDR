OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network, from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

PLEASE TRY
- Receive on a few bands with zoom, Band and the 3D view.
- Your connection type: home network, SmartLink or VPN. Disconnect and reconnect; the spectrum and audio should come back.
- Voice transmit with the device mic or a Bluetooth headset, and tell me how it sounds at the other end. [TX]
- CW keying, CWX memories (F1–F12 on a keyboard) and the decoder. [TX]
- The Log tab: log a few contacts, save your QRZ.com account, share the ADIF file.
- FT8 / FT4 on the FTx tab: decode, drag the red TX band to a free frequency, answer a CQ at low power and check the log. [TX]
- The FreeDV tab: pick a RADE station (14.236 MHz is busy), listen, and Report to FreeDV Reporter. [TX]
- Controls > Digi > PSK31 around 14.070: tap a station, answer its CQ at low power, then Log. And RTTY the same way. [TX]
- New: View > Big buttons and Radio Setup > MIDI.
- AGC panel > Auto on a clear spot.
- The eSSB tab (tube, EVO, reverb) at low power with a second receiver. [TX]

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Right after it happens, open Log in the top row and tap Send to the developer: a mail to oh6oksa@gmail.com opens with the log attached. Add your radio model and what you were doing. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
