OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- FreeDV tab: RADE moved here from the old Digi tab, with FreeDV Reporter: report your station to qso.freedv.org while RADE is on (off by default), and see who is on it, on this band or all bands; tap one to tune there (RADE comes on for a RADE station). Asked by Scott W5SLS.
- The Digi tab is now FTx (FT8 / FT4), and the control buttons are all one size (Prof, Mem).
- RADE: a band button, a frequency on another band or another mode ends RADE first, so no band is left muted in the radio's memory; a band that comes back muted says so (tap the speaker). Thanks Michel F5GNM.
- iPhone Duo and others: the display no longer freezes while iOS reports the app as inactive but it is still on screen; it pauses only in the background. Thanks Vesa OH6HPS.
- DX cluster (View tab): a line under the button says whether it is connected, to which server and as whom, with the spots so far.

RECENT (builds 107–111)
- Mac: clicking a text field opens a small text box to type in (build 111). A swipe in the open controls drawer no longer tunes; PTT in DIGU / DIGL says why it does not transmit; three meters while transmitting.

RECENT (builds 94–106)
- FT8 and FT4 in the app (Controls > FTx): every DIGU slice decodes with WSJT-X colours, RX / TX marks, Keep CQ, Last TX and Free, PSK Reporter; Wavelog / Cloudlog; transverters in Radio Setup; RADE digital voice; the log in Files > On My iPhone / iPad > OksaSDR.

PLEASE TRY
- Receive on a few bands, with zoom, the Band button and the 3D view.
- Your connection type: home network, SmartLink or VPN. Disconnect and reconnect; the spectrum and audio should come back.
- Both portrait and landscape; on iPad also Split View.
- Voice transmit with the device mic or a Bluetooth headset, and tell me how it sounds at the other end. [TX]
- CW keying, CWX memories (F1–F12 on a keyboard) and the decoder. [TX]
- The Log tab: log a few contacts, save your QRZ.com account (it now says if the sign-in worked), and share the ADIF file to your logbook.
- FT8 / FT4 on the FTx tab with two slices in DIGU (e.g. 40 m and 20 m): decode on both, drag the red TX band to a free frequency, answer a CQ at low power and check the contact lands in the log. [TX]
- New: the FreeDV tab: pick a RADE station from the list (14.236 MHz is busy), listen, and turn on Report to FreeDV Reporter to see yourself on qso.freedv.org. [TX]
- New: the log in Files > On My iPhone / iPad > OksaSDR, and Import contacts (ADIF).
- Spots and the DX cluster, a notch on a carrier, the TX processing at low power with a second receiver. [TX]

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Right after it happens, open Log in the top row, tap Copy, and paste the text into an email to oh6oksa@gmail.com. Add your device model, radio model and what you were doing. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
