OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- RTTY transmit on the Digi tab (Controls > Digi, the RTTY tab grown): eight ready macros (CQ, Answer, QSO, BTU, 73 SK, QRZ?, AGN?, Locator; F1–F8 on a keyboard; press and hold to edit, with {HIS}, {NAME} from QRZ.com and {RST} filled in), a field to type and send, Stop (Esc), the calls heard as buttons, and Log. Sent as audio from the TX slice in RTTY, DIGL or DIGU. [TX]
- Switching RTTY / DIGL / DIGU keeps the station tuned in.
- From build 145: RTTY in DIGL / DIGU logged as RTTY on the mark; an RTTY spot keeps DIGL / DIGU.
- From build 144: RTTY receive in the radio's RTTY mode (LSB, the standard), DIGL or DIGU; worked before from Wavelog, the QRZ.com Logbook or a shared ADIF file in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive.
