OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Transmit lock (thanks Larry Averbeck): Controls > TX > Lock. Nothing transmits in standby (SB) until you hold PTT, KEY, Space, TUNE or CQ for a second; then it stays armed (OA) for 5 minutes after your last transmission.
- FTx Answer CQ (thanks Kurt DL5EAG): the app answers CQs by itself, the rarest first, 5 calls each, then the next. It stops after a time without a touch on the app, which you set (1-60 min).
- No burst of band noise at the start of a transmission when the phone's speaker plays receive (thanks Jeff Stinger).
- RTTY decodes the right way round (build 140 needed Reverse); a tap on a signal, an RTTY spot or Autotune tunes onto it; Squelch hides the noise.
- FT8: a QSO always ends with its RR73 or 73 on the air.
- From build 140: Space PTT after typing in the Log panel; RTTY decoding (thanks Ben van Schoor); Mac typing (thanks Pentti OH6PP).
- From build 139: CW Autotune, notches after Add, eSSB, FTx List only (thanks Michael Whaley), VoiceOver names.

PLEASE TRY
- Receive on a few bands, with zoom, Band and the 3D view.
- Your connection type: home network, SmartLink or VPN. Disconnect and reconnect; the spectrum and audio should come back.
- Both portrait and landscape; on iPad also Split View.
- Voice transmit with the device mic or a Bluetooth headset, and tell me how it sounds at the other end. [TX]
- CW keying, CWX memories (F1–F12 on a keyboard) and the decoder. [TX]
- The Log tab: log a few contacts, save your QRZ.com account and share the ADIF file to your logbook.
- FT8 / FT4 on the FTx tab with two slices in DIGU (e.g. 40 m and 20 m): decode on both, drag the red TX band to a free frequency, answer a CQ at low power and check the contact lands in the log. [TX]
- The FreeDV tab: pick a RADE station from the list (14.236 MHz is busy), listen, and turn on Report to FreeDV Reporter to see yourself on qso.freedv.org. [TX]
- New: Lock on the TX tab (a short press does nothing, a second's hold arms); Answer CQ on the FTx tab at low power while you watch [TX]; PTT with the phone's speaker on, and ask whether the start of your transmission is clean [TX]; a tap on an RTTY station.
- Spots and the DX cluster; the eSSB tab at low power with a second receiver. [TX]

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Right after it happens, open Log in the top row, tap Copy, and paste the text into an email to oh6oksa@gmail.com. Add your device model, radio model and what you were doing. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
