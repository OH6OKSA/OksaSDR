OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- The Log tab checks a saved QRZ.com account and Wavelog / Cloudlog key at every start and says whether they still sign in (after a restart it said nothing).

RECENT (build 105)
- iPhone Duo: the app fills the inner screen (since build 104), and in landscape the controls no longer run off the right edge: where they do not fit beside the display they open from Controls. Thank you Vesa OH6HPS, the first Duo user, who made testing on the device possible!
- Wavelog / Cloudlog (Log tab): every QSO you log, by hand or FT8 / FT4, also goes to your online logbook; queued while offline. Save checks the API key and says whether it was accepted.
- PSK Reporter (Digi tab, off by default): reports the stations you decode, as WSJT-X does.
- Transverters in Radio Setup > XVTR: add, edit and remove them.
- FT8 / FT4: a QSO between two nonstandard calls (such as OH6OKSA and SN9Q/QRP) now completes, without reports, as FT8 allows; the green RX mark is always shown.

RECENT (build 104, earlier)
- Built with the iOS 27 SDK: the app fills the iPhone Duo's inner screen. The QSO state also shows at the top of the display; the radio's PROC is off while FT8 / FT4 transmits; a CQ right after a Digi band button no longer stops by itself.

RECENT (builds 94–101)
- FT8 and FT4 in the app (Controls > Digi): every DIGU slice decodes with WSJT-X colours, RX / TX marks, Keep CQ and CQ slot and the New / New DXCC filter, tap a call to answer (it flashes, TX moves to its slice), full power with TX level, AGC by mode; RADE digital voice; the log in Files > On My iPhone / iPad > OksaSDR, ADIF import.

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Right after it happens, open Log in the top row, tap Copy, and paste the text into an email to oh6oksa@gmail.com. Add your device model, radio model and what you were doing. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
