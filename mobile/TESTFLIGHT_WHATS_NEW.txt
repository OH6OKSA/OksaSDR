OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- FM repeater: the offset now moves the transmitter (it sent on the receive frequency before). [TX]
- Mic level before going on the air: choose Mic for the left meter; while Controls -> TX is open it moves in receive too. Nothing is sent before PTT.
- Meters offer only what moves: receive S, Mic, PA temp, Volts; Power, SWR, ALC and Comp in transmit.
- Notch handles are larger and outlined, filled while TNF is on and hollow while off, so they stand out from the waterfall.
- SmartLink keeps the radio's audio registration alive as SmartSDR does; a refused connection is retried.

RECENT (build 84)
- Trackpad scrolling on an iPad keyboard or a Mac; FM repeater offset and CTCSS; 439.000 types as MHz; the active band button is lit.
