OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Dragging the passband tunes in STEP increments, so the frequency ends on a round value (as tapping and the step buttons do).
- Mic level (TX tab) now sets this device's microphone gain, -20 to +30 dB. The radio's mic level never acted on it, so speech could stay low with only a few watts out. Set it so speech peaks near -10 dB on the Mic meter. [TX]
- Clearer buttons: a button that is on is lit blue, the others dark.
- PTT can be a switch: TX tab > PTT > Tap on / off (tap to transmit, tap again to stop; stops by itself after 3 minutes). [TX]
- CW memories and CW text play a sidetone on your device while they are sent.
- Network & performance shows the busiest threads; Android asks for the microphone on the first connect.

RECENT (build 77)
- DX cluster spots on the display (View > Spots); tap one to tune.
- Notch filters (TNF): press and hold on the display; tap a notch to adjust.
- TX tab: processing of this device's microphone (gate, EQ, compressor).
- Memories: press and hold to edit name, frequency and mode. Voice keyer: Listen.
